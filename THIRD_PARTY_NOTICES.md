## Oh My Posh

`themes/bubblesextra.omp.json` (and its embedded copy in `setup_terminal.py`) is a modified version of the
`bubblesextra` theme from [Oh My Posh](https://github.com/JanDeDobbeleer/oh-my-posh).
Changes: layout on one line with a blank line before the prompt, full path display (Linux path in WSL),
recolored to a Catppuccin-based palette, replaced folder and battery glyphs.
